> "May my word be one with my thought,  
> and my thought be one with my word."  
> <cite>-- The Aitareya Upanishad</cite>

Sometimes it's not that easy to ground yourself in stillness, even if you know to your core that stillness is the only way back to peace.

To relieve for a moment the ever tightening ratchet of progress, to allow your forgotten senses to awaken once again.

Breathe.

Come back to the multi-dimensional world you were born into, stop getting caught up in the singular for a while.

Be *alive* for once.
